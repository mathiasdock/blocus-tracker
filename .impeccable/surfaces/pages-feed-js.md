# Activity feed — automatic post bodies

Mode: Operate. Preserve the surrounding feed and all social actions.

Automatic content is event-first, manual content remains caption/photo-first.
Versioned posts.activity drives ActivityPostBody; unknown or legacy content falls back to caption.
Level: dark Blocus green, prominent number medallion. Badge: existing earned artwork.
Streak: count and flame. Sessions/objectives: compact neutral composition.
No always-running celebration, no decorative mascot for ordinary sessions.

Consent is explicit, account-scoped and private. Session posts require at least 60 seconds.
Do not infer typed events from old captions, invent records, expose personal notes or silently enable legacy preferences.
Check 390px mobile and 1440px desktop; retain reactions, comments, visibility, caption editing and delete.
